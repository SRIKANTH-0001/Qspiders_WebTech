MS Dhoni (7)



Mahendra Singh Dhoni (commonly known as MS Dhoni, Mahi, or Thala) is a legendary former captain of the Indian National Cricket Team and the defining icon of the Chennai Super Kings (CSK).
Widely considered one of the greatest cricket captains, Goal-Keeper wicket-keepers, and lower-order finishers in the history of the sport,
he is famous for his tactical brilliance and calm demeanor under extreme pressure, which earned him the nickname "Captain Cool."




⚡Current Status & Ongoing Speculation

Dhoni officially retired from International Cricket on 15 August 2020.
Following severe calf and leg injuries that completely sidelined him from taking the field during the IPL 2026 season,
where he supported CSK purely as a dugout mentor—intense speculation surrounds his active playing future.
CSK management and CEO Kasi Viswanathan have stated that the door is still open for IPL 2027, provided his look fitness permits.
